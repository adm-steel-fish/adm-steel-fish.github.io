Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Steel Fish Studios ("we", "us") makes games, including Crispy the Cat. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit steelfishstudios.com or play our games, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or games, you agree to this policy.

  1. Information We Collect
    • Game saves. If you play without logging in, your progress is saved only on your own device or in your browser. We do not receive it.
    • Player mail. Some games, such as Crispy the Cat, let players send letters to each other using friend codes. To make this work, the game is given a random player ID, a secret key and a friend code, which are stored on our servers along with any display name you choose. Letters you send (sender, recipient, subject, text and time sent) are stored on our servers until the recipient's game receives them, and are then deleted. We also keep a count of how many letters you send each day, and a list of players you have blocked.
    • Discord login. If you choose to log in with Discord, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username, display name and profile picture with us. We do not receive your Discord password or email address. While you are logged in, your game progress (including letters you have received) is stored on our servers so you can continue on any device.
    • Supporter status. If you log in with Discord, we may check which supporter roles (for example, from Ko-fi, Patreon or SubscribeStar) you have in our official Discord server, so we can unlock supporter rewards in our games. We only see those roles, not your payment details.
    • Reports. If a player reports a letter, we keep a copy of that letter, the friend codes of the sender and the reporter, and the reason given, so we can review it.
    • Comments. If you comment on a news article, the name and text you enter are stored and shown publicly on our website.
    • Technical information. To limit abuse, we keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of your IP address along with a daily count of new players created from it; we never store the IP address itself. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type.
    • Payments. We do not process payments ourselves. If you support us on Ko-fi, Patreon or SubscribeStar, those services handle your payment under their own privacy policies, and we do not receive your card details.
  2. How We Use Your Information
    3. We use your information to:
    • Save your game progress and let you continue it on other devices.
    • Deliver letters between players.
    • Unlock supporter rewards for backers.
    • Keep our games safe, for example by filtering inappropriate words, honoring blocks, reviewing reports, and preventing spam.
    • Comply with legal obligations.
    We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  3. Sharing Your Information
    4. We only share information as needed to run our website and games:
    • With service providers that host our website and store our data: Netlify (website hosting) and Google Firebase (database).
    • With Discord, when you choose to log in with it.
    • With other players, who see your display name (or friend code) on letters you send them.
    • If required by law or to protect the safety of our players or our rights.
  4. Data Security and Retention
    5. Our servers are only accessible to Steel Fish Studios, and all data is sent over encrypted connections. We keep information only as long as needed:
    • Letters are deleted from our servers once they are delivered.
    • Unfinished logins expire after 15 minutes. Logging out ends your session.
    • Account data and cloud saves are kept until you ask us to delete them.
    • Reports are kept as long as needed to review them and keep our games safe.
  5. Children's Privacy
    6. Logging in requires a Discord account, and Discord requires its users to be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
  6. Your Rights
    7. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, including your account and cloud save, by emailing info@steelfishstudios.llc. Please include your Discord username or friend code so we can find your data. Residents of certain regions (for example, California or the European Union) may have additional rights under local law.
  7. Changes to This Policy
    8. We may update this policy as our games change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
    Contact Us: For questions, email adm@steelfishstudios.llc or msb@steelfishstudios.llc.
